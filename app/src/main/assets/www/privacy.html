Privacy

Whitestep is built to be used without an account and without tracking.

What stays on your device

Your saved homes and your last search filters are stored in your own browser so they're there next time. They never leave your device, and you can remove them by clearing your browser's site data.

What we receive

If you send us the agency or advertising form, we receive the details you type in, and use them only to reply to you. We don't sell or share them.

Advertising

Sponsored cards are plain links to the sponsor's website. They don't use cookies or track you across sites. When you click one, the sponsor's own privacy policy applies.

Listings

Listings are supplied by Bermuda real estate agencies. When you open a listing, you go to the agency's page, and its privacy policy applies there.

Fonts

Text is displayed with Google Fonts, so your browser requests the font files from Google.
